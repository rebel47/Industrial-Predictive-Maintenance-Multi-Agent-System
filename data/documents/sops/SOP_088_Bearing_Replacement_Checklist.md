# Standard Operating Procedure: Bearing Replacement & Precision Alignment Checklist
**Document ID**: SOP-088-REV3  
**Title**: Rotating Equipment Bearing Overhaul & Alignment Standards  
**Effective Date**: 2023-05-18  
**Applicability**: FlowMax P-100 series pumps and ApexDrive M-200 series motors  

---

## Section 1: Pre-Replacement Verification & Parts Inspection
1. **Verify Replacement Bearing Part Numbers**:
   - For Pump P-104 Drive End (DE): Confirm genuine **SKF 6205-2Z/C3** (or Timken 205-PP-C3). Verify radial internal clearance group C3 ($13\text{--}28\,\mu\text{m}$).
   - Do NOT install standard clearance (CN) bearings in high-temperature slurry pump applications as thermal expansion causes pre-load binding and premature seizure.
2. **Tools & Consumables Required**:
   - Induction bearing heater (do not use open flame or blowtorch).
   - High-temperature synthetic polyurea grease: **Mobil Polyrex EM** (15g initial pack).
   - Replacement elastomeric coupling spider insert (Buna-N 70 Durometer).
   - Dial indicators or laser shaft alignment system.
   - Calibrated torque wrench with 17mm and 19mm sockets.

---

## Section 2: Mounting & Precision Alignment Tolerances
1. **Bearing Heating**:
   - Heat bearing evenly on induction heater to $110^\circ\text{C}\pm 5^\circ\text{C}$. Maximum permissible temperature: $120^\circ\text{C}$.
   - Slide bearing squarely against the shaft locating shoulder. Hold firmly until cool to avoid axial gap.
2. **Coupling Alignment Standards (1750 RPM)**:
   - **Parallel Offset Misalignment**: Maximum allowable $\le 0.05\text{ mm}$ ($2.0\text{ mils}$).
   - **Angular Misalignment**: Maximum allowable $\le 0.04\text{ mm}$ per 100mm coupling diameter.
3. **Torque Specifications**:
   - Pump casing foundation hold-down bolts: $140\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$.
   - Bearing housing cap bolts: $65\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$.
